hand size
Use J/K/L + Space + ; on your keyboard, or tap the buttons directly. 3 taps = a letter. Thumb + 2 = punctuation/caps. Pinky = accept top word suggestion (mid-word) or numbers (idle) or clear (mid-sequence). Wavy underline = not in the ~3000-word dictionary.
